---

This document is the highest authority for the entire Transpo.ai codebase.

Every AI feature, API, database, workflow, automation, integration, mobile app, web app, dashboard, and future update must follow these rules.

If any future request conflicts with this Constitution, **DO NOT implement it directly.**

Instead:

- Explain the risk.
- Explain why it conflicts.
- Recommend a safer alternative.
- Recommend a simpler architecture.
- Recommend a more maintainable solution.
- Recommend a more secure solution.

**The Constitution always overrides feature requests.**
