## OUR MISSION

Build the world's most trusted AI-powered trucking platform.

- We build software.
- We do not operate trucking companies.
- We assist businesses.
- We never replace human responsibility.

## OUR ROLE

Transpo.ai is an intelligent assistant.

Not a dispatcher.
Not a lawyer.
Not an accountant.
Not a payroll administrator.
Not a compliance officer.
Not a safety officer.
Not a government representative.
Not an insurance advisor.
Not a decision maker.

**Humans always remain responsible.**

## AI PHILOSOPHY

- AI assists. Humans decide.
- AI prepares. Humans approve.
- AI explains. Humans remain responsible.

- Medium

## LOW MAINTENANCE ENGINEERING

Every feature should reduce maintenance.

- Reduce support tickets.
- Reduce clicks.
- Reduce repetitive work.
- Prefer modular architecture.
- Prefer reusable components.
- Prefer configuration over customization.
- Avoid unnecessary complexity.
- Avoid technical debt.
- Avoid duplicate code.

## SECURITY

- Least privilege.
- Encryption.
- Audit logs.
- Role permissions.
- Version history.
- Secure authentication.
- Protect customer data.
- Protect driver data.
- Protect business data.

## CUSTOMER CONTROL

- Customers own their data.
- Customers choose automations.
- Customers enable integrations.
- Customers approve critical actions.
- Customers remain responsible for operating their business.

## ENGINEERING STANDARD

Before building every feature ask:

1. Is it safer?
2. Is it simpler?
3. Is it easier to maintain?
4. Is it easier to scale?
5. Is it more secure?
6. Does it reduce manual work?
7. Does it reduce clicks?
8. Does it reduce support?
9. Would it still be the correct solution in 10 years?

If not — **redesign.**

## LONG TERM THINKING

Build for decades.

Not demos.
Not hype.
Not temporary trends.

Build software customers trust every day.

- Trust is more important than AI.
